--- RTX 4060 Laptop (Ada Lovelace SM 8.9) --- [Local machine]
Stage 1  FP32 Baseline : 14.9 tok/s | Mem BW 58.1 GB/s | Occupancy 16.6% | L2 hit 0.86%
Stage 2  Naive INT8    : 16.1 tok/s | Mem BW 19.2 GB/s | Occupancy 16.6% | L2 hit 2.63%
Stage 3  Warp-Opt INT8 : 31.8 tok/s | Mem BW 85.6 GB/s | Occupancy 86.8% | L2 hit 7.80%
Stage 4  Fused Ops     : 30.0 tok/s
Stage 5  Flash Attn    : 35.2 tok/s
Stage 6  dp4a W8A8     : ~44 tok/s (avg: 45.56, 42.33, 42.22, 43.70)
           vs Stage 5: +27% end-to-end gain
           PPL verification (2026-05-19, stride=2048, WikiText-2):
             FP16 Baseline : 13.1984
             W8A32         : 13.2188  (+0.0204, +0.15%)
             W8A8 Stage 6  : 13.3610  (+0.1626, +1.23% vs FP16)
           Conclusion: W8A8 dp4a adds only 1.23% PPL degradation for +27% throughput
