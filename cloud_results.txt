SECTION 1: RTX 4060 LAPTOP — Main Ablation (1B model)
Ada Lovelace SM 8.9 | 8GB GDDR6 | 272 GB/s peak BW
===================================================

--- End-to-End Decoding Throughput (tok/s) ---
Stage 1  FP32 Baseline : 14.9 tok/s
Stage 2  Naive INT8    : 16.1 tok/s
Stage 3  Warp-Opt INT8 : 31.8 tok/s
Stage 4  Fused Ops     : 30.0 tok/s
Stage 5  Flash Attn    : 35.2 tok/s
Stage 6  dp4a W8A8     : 44.0 tok/s  (runs: 45.56, 42.33, 42.22, 43.70; σ=1.5)
  Cumulative Stage1→6 : 2.95× (14.9 → 44.0)
  Stage5→6 gain       : 1.25× (+25%)

Key findings:
- Stage2 L1 hit 96.8% (higher than Stage1!) but throughput worse → L2/DRAM coalescing is bottleneck
- Stage3→6: occupancy same (86.8%), IPC +45% (1.43→2.08), Mem BW -96% (31.5%→1.19%)
- Stage6 is compute-bound, all other stages are memory-bound

SECTION 4: 3B MODEL VALIDATION
RTX 3090 (Ampere SM 8.6, 936 GB/s, 24GB VRAM) [Vast.ai 2026-05-20]
Model: Llama-3.2-3B (d=3072, 28 layers, 24 heads, 8 KV heads)
===================================================

Stage 1 FP32       :  8.05 tok/s avg (runs: 8.08, 8.08, 8.00, 8.04) | TPOT ~124 ms/tok
Stage 5 Flash Attn : 28.71 tok/s | TPOT 34.83 ms/tok
Stage 6 dp4a W8A8  : 35.07 tok/s (runs: 35.09, 35.06, 35.07, 35.06) | TPOT ~28.5 ms/tok

Cumulative Stage1→6 : 35.07 / 8.05 = 4.36×  (vs 1B: 2.95×)
Stage 5→6 ratio     : 1.22×                  (vs 1B: 1.25×)  ← consistent

Deployment implication:
  3B FP32 weights = 12 GB → OOM on RTX 4060 (8 GB)
  3B INT8 weights ≈ 3 GB  → fits comfortably on 8 GB edge GPU
  → INT8 quantization is an ENABLER for 3B on edge, not just an optimization

Engineering note:
  int32 overflow bug fixed: w1/w2/w3 cudaMalloc needed size_t cast
  28 × 3072 × 8192 × 4 = 2.82B bytes > INT_MAX (2.15B)
